About us

Local, reliable, and easy to trust with your keys.

Bright & Tidy started as a two-person team cleaning houses around Stafford. Today we're a small, closely-managed crew who still treat every job like it's our own home.

🛡️

Fully insured

Every clean is covered by public liability insurance, so you're protected as well as your home.

✅

DBS-checked team

All cleaners pass a background check before they're ever given a set of keys.

🌿

Eco-conscious products

We default to low-chemical, biodegradable products — tell us if you'd like something different.

🤝

Same cleaner each visit

For recurring bookings, we try to keep the same cleaner assigned so they get to know your home.

Our story

Why we started Bright & Tidy

We noticed that a lot of local cleaning services were hard to book, slow to reply, and inconsistent about who actually turned up. Bright & Tidy was built to fix that: a small, well-managed team, clear pricing, and a booking process that takes minutes instead of phone tag.

We now work with households across Stafford as well as a handful of local offices, and we keep our client list small enough that every customer still gets a real reply from a real person.

Want to know more before you book?

Get in touch and we'll answer any questions about scheduling, products, or pricing.
